# 首页卡片插画

## 当前版本 0.0.58

运行资源为用户确认的 `Resources/CardArtwork.png` 图集。通过内置 imagegen 生成并迭代，最后一轮只将第三张改为“轮盘选中位置 → 放大的 prompt 卡片”，保留截图七个工具图标、两行窗口缩略图和拼屏布局。

原生 `SketchCardArt` 使用固定源区域呈现四张插画，不把图集自带的卡片边框、中文标题烘焙进入口；真实标题、hover、开关和功能池操作保持原生。图集只读取一次，派生为小尺寸缓存，不保留整张解码图。

提示词约束：near-white paper; thin irregular graphite outlines; light crayon blue/green/lavender; no characters, thick solid black border, camera lens or generic chat bubble. Capture shows crop handles and a seven-icon toolbar. Switching shows six thumbnail cards in two rows and one selected border. Prompt library shows one selected wheel segment connected to a enlarged note labeled exactly `prompt`. Layout shows three panes filling one monitor.

## CardCapture

A big blue crayon screenshot frame: softly uneven rounded blue square with dark graphite outline, crop-corner marks extending slightly beyond its top-left and bottom-right edges, one dark circular camera lens in the center. A tiny coral-red recording dot in the upper right, understated. Simple single cohesive symbol.

Production bitmap asset: one native macOS toolbox entry-card illustration for macos-x. Match a warm naive childlike crayon doodle illustration, dense overlapping wax crayon pigment strokes with visible white paper gaps, clear rough graphite pencil outlines with little broken retraces, slightly irregular handdrawn geometry. Sophisticated through minimal composition. Frontal flat square composition, subject centered and LARGE, fills 85 percent of canvas width and height with tight transparent margins. No enclosing tile, no outer rounded-square background, no poster, no people, no faces, no words, no lettering, no numbers, no decorative stars, no shadows outside the symbol, no glossy surfaces, no gradients, no polished vector artwork, no 3D. Genuine transparent PNG background. Strong instantly readable shape at 100 logical pixels. The entire illustration should use the same thick-but-grainy graphite line and wax-paper texture as a colored crayon bitten-apple app icon.
